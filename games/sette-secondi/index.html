How to play Seven Seconds

Seven Seconds is the 7 second challenge as a free online game: start the stopwatch, count in your head and stop it at exactly 7.00 seconds. 3 rounds, points for every hundredth you get right, and a Blind mode where the dial goes dark after 2 seconds.

  1. Tap PLAY: the stopwatch is ready at 0.00.
  2. Tap once to start the timer.
  3. Tap again when you think exactly 7 seconds have passed.
  4. The closer you are to 7.00, the more points you score; a perfect stop earns a bonus.
  5. Play 3 rounds, then try Blind mode.

FAQ

What is the 7 second challenge?

A timing game: you start a stopwatch and try to stop it at exactly 7.00 seconds. It tests your inner clock and your reflexes.

How do I get a perfect score?

Count steadily in your head and tap on the beat. A stop within a hundredth of a second counts as perfect and earns a bonus.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. At the end of a game tap Challenge to send a link with your score. There is also a weekly leaderboard.

Do I need an account?

No. On the leaderboard you appear with an anonymous name (an animal and a number), or with a nickname you choose at the end of a game.
