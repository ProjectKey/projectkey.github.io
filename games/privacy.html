What we collect

We do not ask you to sign up and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or location. To run leaderboards and statistics we use:

  • an anonymous install code, randomly generated and stored in your browser (local storage), to recognise games played on the same device and count returning players;
  • on Telegram, the numeric ID of your account, verified with the signature Telegram provides to the Mini App. We do not store your name, username or photo;
  • game statistics: events such as "game started" or "score", with game version, language and platform. We do not store your IP address: we only use it, in a non-reversible form and for one day, to prevent abuse.

Why

To run the games (leaderboards, challenges, best scores) and to improve them with aggregate statistics. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. There are no profiling cookies.

Where and for how long

Data is stored on Supabase servers in the European Union (Ireland). Statistical events are deleted after 90 days. Open, unfinished game sessions expire after 2 hours.

On the leaderboard

You appear with a generated name (an animal and a number), never with data that identifies you.

Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of your data by writing to supporto@settegames.com. The anonymous code is in your browser settings (the site's local storage); if you delete it we can no longer link earlier games to your device. You can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or the authority of your country.

Children

The games are suitable for everyone (PEGI 3), contain no purchases or personalised advertising and do not collect personal data.
