Last Sheep Royale — Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · version 1.3.0 (ads, Google Play Games)

In short. The game creates a guest account for you on first launch, without asking anything: your coins, gems and progress are kept on our server, in the EU. You can link Facebook later if you want; then we receive your Facebook ID for this app, your public name and, if you allow it on Facebook's screen, your email (never your password), and we don't keep your Facebook token. If your phone uses Google Play Games, the game links your account to it by itself, without asking for any permission: we receive only your Play Games player ID, never your email, and we don't keep Google's tokens. Your friends in the game see your sheep's name, look, level, league, weekly coins and wins. Gameplay statistics stay anonymous and are not linked to your account. Google shows ads (with your consent where the law requires it) and handles payments. We don't sell data. You can delete your account at any time.

If you link Facebook

Linking Facebook is optional: it saves your progress so you can play on a new phone, and finds friends who play. The login happens on Facebook's page; we never see your password. With Facebook we receive and keep:

  • your Facebook ID for this app (an ID that Facebook creates only for Last Sheep Royale);
  • your public profile: name and profile picture, as Facebook gives them with the public_profile permission;
  • your email address, only if you allow it on Facebook's screen (email permission; you can untick it). We use it only to identify your account and to answer you about it, never for marketing;
  • only if you allow it (user_friends permission): the list of your Facebook friends who also play and linked Facebook. We use it when you log in to make them your friends in the game; we keep the friendships, not the list.

We don't keep your Facebook token: it is used once, at login, and thrown away. We don't ask for your email, and we never post anything on Facebook. What Meta does with your login is described in Meta's privacy policy.

If your phone uses Google Play Games, the game links your account to it by itself, without asking for any permission, so you get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling. If that Play Games player already has an account and this phone has guest progress, you choose which one to keep. If Play Games isn't signed in, nothing is linked and you stay a guest; Settings → ACCOUNT → CONNECT tries again.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner outside races, on the game's menus, and video ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward. There are no ads during a race. No Ads, a one-time purchase, removes the banner; reward videos stay, always optional.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, the ad software's own diagnostic and crash data, and how you interact with ads, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google does this as an independent controller, under its own policies.

Legal basis. Personalised ads, and reading or storing the advertising ID and other information on your phone for ads, are based on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and art. 122 of the Italian privacy code). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for it with Google's consent form (UMP) before any ad is requested. If you say no, you may get non-personalised ads, based only on context (the app, the approximate area from the IP address): these are shown on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game free, and only to the extent your choices in the form allow; otherwise you get no ads. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy; this doesn't affect what was done before. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. Our own statistics never use the advertising ID. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

Gem packs and No Ads are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment: we never see your card or your Google account. The game receives the confirmation of the purchase (which pack, and a purchase code) and sends it to our server with your account, which asks Google whether it is valid and then adds the gems to your account.

The server keeps the purchase code, the pack, Google's order number, the installation ID, your account and the date, so that one purchase can't be used twice and so we can help if you report a problem (legitimate interest: fraud prevention). To limit abuse, the server keeps your IP address in memory only, for ten minutes. If you delete your account, we keep only the purchase code, the pack, Google's order number, the status and the dates, with no link to you or to your device: they stop the same purchase from being credited twice, and accounting needs them.

Notifications

Reminders (a chest is ready, the daily gift) are scheduled on your phone. No notification server is involved. You can turn each kind off in Settings, or all of them in Android settings.

What we don't collect

No phone number, contacts from your phone, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The app doesn't ask for those permissions, and they are explicitly blocked in its configuration. Inside the app we don't receive any email: the only one we may have is your Google account's, if you shared it with an earlier version of the app (see Google Play Games above). If you write to us, we get the address you write from, used only to answer you. We don't keep your Facebook token or read your Facebook posts.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete account, and confirm twice. Your account, game, friendships and race results are deleted right away.

On the web: settegames.com/lastsheep/delete.html. If you linked Facebook, log in there and delete. Otherwise (guest or Google Play Games), email supporto@settegames.com with your account code (six characters, in Settings).

From Facebook: remove Last Sheep Royale in your Facebook settings (Apps and websites). Facebook tells us, we delete the account, and you get a link to check it's done.

Google Play Games: deleting the account removes the Play Games link, and your email if you had shared it. To also remove the game's access from your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/connections.

What stays: the purchase code, pack, Google order number, status and dates of a purchase, with no link to you or your device. The anonymous statistics are not linked to your account: to have them erased too, send us the installation ID shown in Settings under the credits. We delete every record with that ID within thirty days, error reports included.

Backups. For safety we keep a few copies of the database on a company computer, not shared with anyone. Deleted data disappears from them when the copies are replaced: we keep only the last 14 copies and destroy the older ones.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or erase your data, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent (personalised ads), you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy, without affecting the processing done before. Write to the contact address with your account code. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the one where you live.

Children

The game is meant for players aged at least 14 (the age of digital consent in Italy, art. 2-quinquies of the Italian privacy code and art. 8 GDPR), or the age of digital consent in your country if higher, and is not directed at children. It contains ads and real-money purchases. Facebook Login and Google Play Games sign-in have the same minimum age. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age; if we find out we did, we delete the account.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top is always the latest version.

Se dici no, puoi ricevere annunci non personalizzati, basati solo sul contesto (l'app, l'area approssimativa dall'indirizzo IP): li mostriamo sulla base del nostro legittimo interesse a tenere il gioco gratuito, e solo nei limiti delle scelte fatte nel modulo; altrimenti non ricevi annunci. Puoi revocare o cambiare il consenso quando vuoi da Settings → Ad privacy, senza effetti su quanto fatto prima. Puoi anche azzerare o eliminare l'identificativo pubblicitario dalle impostazioni del telefono. Le nostre statistiche non usano mai l'identificativo pubblicitario. Come Google usa i dati: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Acquisti (Google Play)

I pacchetti di gemme e No Ads si comprano tramite Google Play. Il pagamento lo gestisce Google: non vediamo mai la tua carta né il tuo account Google. Il gioco riceve la conferma dell'acquisto (quale pacchetto e un codice d'acquisto) e la manda al nostro server insieme al tuo account; il server chiede a Google se è valido e poi aggiunge le gemme al tuo account.

Il server conserva codice, pacchetto, numero d'ordine di Google, identificativo d'installazione, account e data, perché lo stesso acquisto non si usi due volte e per aiutarti se segnali un problema (legittimo interesse: prevenzione delle frodi). Per limitare gli abusi il server tiene l'indirizzo IP solo in memoria, per dieci minuti. Quando cancelli l'account, delle ricevute d'acquisto di Google Play conserviamo solo il codice della ricevuta, il prodotto, il numero d'ordine, lo stato e le date, senza nessun collegamento a te né al tuo dispositivo: servono a impedire che la stessa ricevuta venga accreditata due volte e per la contabilità.

Notifiche

I promemoria (un baule pronto, il regalo del giorno) sono programmati sul telefono. Non c'è un server di notifiche. Puoi spegnerli uno per uno nelle impostazioni del gioco, o tutti da quelle di Android.

Cosa non raccogliamo

Numero di telefono, rubrica del telefono, foto, microfono, fotocamera, posizione precisa: niente di questo. L'app non chiede quei permessi, e sono bloccati esplicitamente nella sua configurazione. Dentro all'app non riceviamo nessuna email: l'unica che possiamo avere è quella del tuo account Google, se l'avevi condivisa con una versione precedente dell'app (vedi Google Play Games sopra). Se ci scrivi, riceviamo l'indirizzo da cui scrivi, usato solo per risponderti. Non conserviamo il token di Facebook e non leggiamo i tuoi post.

Cancellare l'account e i dati

Nell'app: Settings → Delete account, e conferma due volte. Account, gioco, amicizie ed esiti delle corse si cancellano subito.

Dal web: settegames.com/lastsheep/delete.html. Se hai collegato Facebook, accedi lì e cancella. Altrimenti (ospite o Google Play Games), scrivi a supporto@settegames.com con il tuo codice account (sei caratteri, in Settings).

Da Facebook: togli Last Sheep Royale dalle impostazioni di Facebook (App e siti web). Facebook ce lo dice, cancelliamo l'account, e ricevi un link per controllare che sia fatto.

Google Play Games: cancellando l'account si cancellano anche il collegamento a Play Games e la tua email, se l'avevi condivisa. Per togliere anche l'accesso del gioco dal tuo account Google: myaccount.google.com/connections.

Cosa resta: codice della ricevuta, prodotto, numero d'ordine, stato e date di un acquisto, senza legame con te né col tuo dispositivo. Le statistiche anonime non sono legate all'account: per far cancellare anche quelle, mandaci l'identificativo d'installazione che trovi in Settings sotto ai crediti. Cancelliamo ogni riga con quell'ID entro trenta giorni, segnalazioni di errore comprese.

Copie di sicurezza. Per sicurezza teniamo alcune copie del database su un computer della società, non condivise con nessuno. I dati cancellati spariscono anche da lì quando le copie vengono sostituite: teniamo solo le ultime 14 copie e distruggiamo le più vecchie.

I tuoi diritti

Puoi chiedere di accedere ai tuoi dati, correggerli, cancellarli, limitarne il trattamento, opporti, e riceverli in un formato leggibile da una macchina. Dove ci basiamo sul tuo consenso (annunci personalizzati), puoi revocarlo quando vuoi da Settings → Ad privacy, senza effetti sul trattamento fatto prima. Scrivi all'indirizzo di contatto con il tuo codice account. Puoi anche rivolgerti al Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it) o all'autorità del paese in cui vivi.

Minori

Il gioco è pensato per chi ha almeno 14 anni (l'età del consenso digitale in Italia, art. 2-quinquies del Codice privacy e art. 8 GDPR), o l'età del consenso digitale del tuo paese se è più alta, e non si rivolge ai bambini: contiene pubblicità e acquisti con denaro vero. Anche l'accesso con Facebook e con Google Play Games ha la stessa età minima. Non raccogliamo consapevolmente dati di chi è sotto quell'età; se ce ne accorgiamo, cancelliamo l'account.
