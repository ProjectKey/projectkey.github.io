Last Sheep Royale — Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · for the version with game accounts

In short. The game creates a guest account for you on first launch, without asking anything: your coins, gems and progress are kept on our server, in the EU. You can link Facebook later if you want; then we receive your Facebook ID for this app and your public name, never your email or your password, and we don't keep your Facebook token. Your friends in the game see your sheep's name, look, level, league, weekly coins and wins. Gameplay statistics stay anonymous and are not linked to your account. Google shows ads (with your consent where the law requires it) and handles payments. We don't sell data. You can delete your account at any time.

If you link Facebook

Linking Facebook is optional: it saves your progress so you can play on a new phone, and finds friends who play. The login happens on Facebook's page; we never see your password. With Facebook we receive and keep:

  • your Facebook ID for this app (an ID that Facebook creates only for Last Sheep Royale);
  • your public profile: name and profile picture, as Facebook gives them with the public_profile permission;
  • only if you allow it (user_friends permission): the list of your Facebook friends who also play and linked Facebook. We use it when you log in to make them your friends in the game; we keep the friendships, not the list.

We don't keep your Facebook token: it is used once, at login, and thrown away. We don't ask for your email, and we never post anything on Facebook. What Meta does with your login is described in Meta's privacy policy.

What your friends see

Your friends in the game see: your sheep's name and look (wool, face, hat), your level, league, coins won this week and wins. Nothing else: not your Facebook profile, not your coins or gems.

When you share your invite link, whoever opens it sees your sheep's name and look, and your code. Don't share it if you don't want that.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner on the home screen and video ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward. There are no ads during a race.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information and how you interact with ads, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent (Google's consent form) before any ad is requested; without it you only get non-personalised ads, or none. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

Gem packs and No Ads are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment: we never see your card or your Google account. The game receives the confirmation of the purchase (which pack, and a purchase code) and sends it to our server with your account, which asks Google whether it is valid and then adds the gems to your account.

The server keeps the purchase code, the pack, Google's order number, the installation ID, your account and the date, so that one purchase can't be used twice and so we can help if you report a problem (legitimate interest: fraud prevention). To limit abuse, the server keeps your IP address in memory only, for ten minutes. If you delete your account, we keep only the purchase code, the pack, Google's order number, the status and the dates, with no link to you or to your device: they stop the same purchase from being credited twice, and accounting needs them.

Notifications

Reminders (a chest is ready, the daily gift) are scheduled on your phone. No notification server is involved. You can turn each kind off in Settings, or all of them in Android settings.

What we don't collect

No email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The app doesn't ask for those permissions, and they are explicitly blocked in its configuration. We don't keep your Facebook token or read your Facebook posts.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete account, and confirm twice. Your account, game, friendships and race results are deleted right away.

On the web: projectkey.github.io/lastsheep/delete.html. If you linked Facebook, log in there and delete. If you are a guest, email supporto.applicazioni@gmail.com with your account code (six characters, in Settings).

From Facebook: remove Last Sheep Royale in your Facebook settings (Apps and websites). Facebook tells us, we delete the account, and you get a link to check it's done.

What stays: the purchase code, pack, Google order number, status and dates of a purchase, with no link to you or your device. The anonymous statistics are not linked to your account: to have them erased too, send us the installation ID shown in Settings under the credits. We delete every record with that ID within thirty days, crash reports included.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or erase your data, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Write to the contact address with your account code. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the one where you live.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It contains ads and real-money purchases. Facebook Login has the same minimum age.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top is always the latest version.

Il gioco mostra un banner nella schermata principale e video solo quando scegli tu di guardarne uno in cambio di un premio. Durante la corsa non c'è pubblicità.

Nello Spazio economico europeo, nel Regno Unito e in Svizzera ti chiediamo il consenso (il modulo di Google) prima di richiedere qualunque annuncio; senza, ricevi solo annunci non personalizzati, o nessuno. Acquisti (Google Play)

I pacchetti di gemme e No Ads si comprano tramite Google Play. Il pagamento lo gestisce Google: non vediamo mai la tua carta né il tuo account Google. Il gioco riceve la conferma dell'acquisto (quale pacchetto e un codice d'acquisto) e la manda al nostro server insieme al tuo account; il server chiede a Google se è valido e poi aggiunge le gemme al tuo account.

Il server conserva codice, pacchetto, numero d'ordine di Google, identificativo d'installazione, account e data, perché lo stesso acquisto non si usi due volte e per aiutarti se segnali un problema (legittimo interesse: prevenzione delle frodi). Per limitare gli abusi il server tiene l'indirizzo IP solo in memoria, per dieci minuti. Quando cancelli l'account, delle ricevute d'acquisto di Google Play conserviamo solo il codice della ricevuta, il prodotto, il numero d'ordine, lo stato e le date, senza nessun collegamento a te né al tuo dispositivo: servono a impedire che la stessa ricevuta venga accreditata due volte e per la contabilità.
