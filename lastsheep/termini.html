Last Sheep Royale — Terms of service

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · version 1.3.0 (ads, No Ads, Google Play Games)

In short. Last Sheep Royale is a racing game. The coins and gems inside it are not money: they can't be cashed out, traded or converted. They are kept on our server, in your game account, so the game needs an internet connection. The only things sold for real money are gem packs and No Ads, through Google Play.

Accepting these terms

By using the game you accept these terms and the privacy policy. If you don't accept them, don't use the game.

Who can play

You must be at least 14, the age from which you can consent to online services yourself in Italy (art. 2-quinquies of the Italian privacy code, art. 8 GDPR), or the age of digital consent in your country if higher. If you are under 18, ask a parent before buying anything. The game contains chests with random rewards.

Your account

On first launch the game creates a guest account for you. If your phone uses Google Play Games, the game links the account to it by itself, without asking for any permission. You can also link Facebook, from Settings. With either one you can play the same account on another phone. Without them, a guest account lives on one phone: if you lose the phone or uninstall the game, we can't give it back to you. One person, one account is enough; you may not sell or give away accounts.

If you log in with a Play Games player or a Facebook account that already has a game account, you choose which one to keep. The two are not merged. If you move to that account, the gems you bought as a guest come with you.

You can delete your account at any time: in the app (Settings → Delete account) or on the deletion page. Deleting is final: coins, gems, items and friends are gone, including those bought with real money.

Internet connection

The game needs an internet connection to start and to spend. Without it you see «No connection». A race that has already started always ends; its result is sent when the connection is back, within 24 hours.

The other runners, and your friends

The other sheep in a race and the rivals in the weekly league are controlled by the computer. We say so because it's true, not because it changes anything: you play against the same rules they do.

Your friends are real people: you add them with an invite link, a code or Facebook, and you can remove them from the list. The friends leaderboard counts the coins won in the week.

Coins, gems and items

Coins, gems, chests, the pass, skins and every other in-game item are a licence to use them inside the game, not property and not credit. They have no monetary value, can't be sold, traded or moved to another account. They are kept on our server, in your game account, and what the server says is what you have. They are lost if you delete the account or reset your progress, and a guest account without Google Play Games or Facebook is lost with its phone — including those bought with real money.

Purchases

Gem packs and No Ads are bought through Google Play, at the price Google shows you at the moment of purchase, taxes included. We never see your payment details. No Ads removes the banner for good on your Google account; reward videos stay, always optional.

Right of withdrawal. Gems and No Ads are digital content delivered immediately. By buying them you ask for immediate delivery and accept that, once delivered, the fourteen-day right of withdrawal no longer applies (art. 59 of the Italian Consumer Code, art. 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU).

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its rules. If you paid and didn't receive what you bought, write to us: if the mistake is ours, we fix it.

Advertising

The game is free partly thanks to ads: a banner outside races, on the game's menus, and videos you choose to watch for a reward. No video ever starts on its own, and there are no ads during a race. A reward is given only when the video is watched to the end. In Europe we ask for your consent first; see the privacy policy.

Fair play

Don't modify the app, use tools that play for you, send fake race results, or exploit bugs to multiply coins, gems or rewards. The server checks every result: a result that can't be real is cut to what is possible, and accounts that do it often get smaller rewards and can be left out of the friends leaderboards. We may also remove what was obtained unfairly.

The game changes

Rewards, in-game prices, seasons and rules can change, and some things may be removed. If a change affects something you bought with real money, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. If we ever stop the game, we'll announce it in the app at least thirty days ahead and stop selling gems right away.

When it doesn't work

We do our best, but can't promise the game will always work: an update can contain a mistake, our server or your connection can be down. Your consumer rights under Italian and EU law are never limited by these terms. Beyond them, we are not liable for indirect damage, such as progress lost to a fault.

Law and courts
