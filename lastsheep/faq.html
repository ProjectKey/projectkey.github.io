Questions and answers

Not here? Settings → CONTACT US in the game, or supporto@settegames.com.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Your coins, gems and progress live on our server, so the game needs the internet to start and to spend. A race that has already started finishes even if the connection drops: the result is sent when you are back online (within 24 hours).

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

With Google Play Games: on the new phone, use the same Play Games player and open the game. Your account comes back by itself.

With Facebook: link it first, Settings → ACCOUNT → SAVE WITH FACEBOOK. On the new phone, tap Already playing? Log in in the same place. Level, coins, gems, items and friends come back.

Google Play Games sign-in

If your phone uses Play Games, the game saves your account there by itself, with no permission to give: we don't get your email, only your Play Games player ID. Play Games not signed in? You stay a guest; Settings → ACCOUNT → CONNECT tries again. If that player already has a sheep, you pick one: this phone or Play Games. LOG OUT or DELETE ACCOUNT are in the same place.

What is a guest account?

Everyone starts as a guest: nothing to fill in. A guest account is tied to this phone. If you uninstall the game or clear its data without Google Play Games or Facebook linked, you can't get back in. Your 6-letter account code is in Settings → ACCOUNT: keep it, and send it to us if you need help.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: Settings → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT, confirmed twice. It is final. You can also do it on this page, with Facebook or by email.

I paid but got nothing

Close the game and open it again with the internet on: purchases are checked at start-up and delivered then. Still missing? Write to us with your account code and the Google order number (it starts with GPA., it's in your Google Play receipt email). Refunds go through Google Play.

I'm out of coins

The Not enough coins panel gives three ways: GAMES (the daily games), WATCH a video for coins for two races, or the SHOP. The shop's FREE section also has videos, three a day.

I watched a video and got no reward

The reward comes only when the video plays to the end. If it doesn't load, is closed early or the connection drops, nothing is given: try again a bit later. Free videos in the shop have a daily limit; it resets the next day.

How do I add friends?

Open Friends and tap INVITE to send your link, or ENTER CODE to type a friend's 6-letter code. Your code is the same as your account code. Long-press a friend to remove them.

How does the leaderboard work?

Points are the coins you win in races this week, not coins you buy. In your league of 20, the top five move up and the bottom five move down when the week ends. The Friends list is ranked the same way and starts again every Monday.

What does No Ads do?

It removes the banner, for good, on your Google account. Videos stay: they are never forced, you watch one only when you choose to for a reward. After reinstalling, No Ads comes back by itself once you open the game with the same Google account.

The game crashed or something looks wrong

Settings → CONTACT US. The email already contains your account code, game version and phone model: just tell us what you were doing.
