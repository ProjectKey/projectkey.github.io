Snake 100 Battle Royale — Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Android and iPhone (App Store, Sign in with Apple)

In short. On first launch the game creates a guest account for you, without asking anything. Your coins, gems and progress are kept on our server, in the EU, and purchases are checked there. If your phone uses Google Play Games, the game links your account to it by itself, without asking for any permission: we receive only your Play Games player ID. You can also log in with Facebook or, on iPhone, with Apple, if you want: it saves your account for a new phone. With Facebook we get your Facebook ID for our app, your name and picture and, if you allow it, your email. With Apple only an Apple ID for our app. Gameplay statistics stay anonymous. Google shows ads (with your consent where the law requires it); Google Play or the App Store handle payments; Expo delivers small game updates. We don't sell data. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Google Play Games

If your phone is signed in to Google Play Games, the game links your account to it at launch, by itself, without asking for any permission. If you reinstall or change phone, your account comes back when you open the game, before you play your first match there. If Play Games isn't signed in, you stay a guest.

The game asks Play Games only for a one-time code, with no email or profile permission. Our server exchanges it with Google, reads your Play Games player ID and throws the rest away. We don't keep Google's tokens, and we never receive your email, name, contacts or anything else. We never post anything.

We keep the player ID with your account, as long as the account exists. To sign you back in on a new phone, our server may give your account an internal address like pg-…@play.invalid, made from a scrambled code of the player ID. It is not a real email: no message can be sent to it. Legal basis: contract. Google provides Play Games under its own privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner on the home screen, and video ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward. There are no ads during a match. No Ads, a one-time purchase, removes the banner; reward videos stay, always optional.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, the ad software's own diagnostic and crash data, and how you interact with ads, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google does this as an independent controller, under its own policies. With a reward video the game also passes AdMob your account ID and a one-time code, only so that AdMob can confirm to our server that the video was watched.

Legal basis. Personalised ads, and reading or storing the advertising ID and other information on your phone for ads, are based on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and art. 122 of the Italian privacy code). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for it with Google's consent form (UMP) before any ad is requested, and never during your first match. If you say no, you may get non-personalised ads, based only on context (the app, the approximate area from the IP address): these are shown on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game free, and only to the extent your choices in the form allow; otherwise you get no ads. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time in Settings → AD PRIVACY (shown when the form applies to you); this doesn't affect what was done before. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

On iPhone. Before any ad, after Google's form, the game shows Apple's tracking request (App Tracking Transparency). AdMob reads the advertising ID (IDFA) only if you tap Allow; if you don't, you get non-personalised ads and no IDFA. You can change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ad networks may also measure installs with Apple's SKAdNetwork, which gives them only aggregated, anonymous counts.

Purchases (Google Play and App Store)

Gem packs, the Starter Pack, the premium season pass and No Ads are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment: we never see your card or your Google account. The game tells Google your account ID, so the purchase belongs to your account. After you pay, the game sends the confirmation (which pack, and a purchase code) to our server, which asks Google whether it is valid and only then adds what you bought.

The server keeps the purchase code, the pack, Google's order number, the installation ID, your account, the status and the date, so that one purchase can't be used twice and so we can help if you report a problem (legitimate interest: fraud prevention). It keeps your IP address in memory only, for ten minutes, to limit abuse. If Google refunds a purchase, we take back what it gave.

On iPhone you buy through the App Store: Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card or your Apple ID. The game tells Apple your account ID (as a random code), gets Apple's signed receipt (which pack, the transaction ID), and our server checks Apple's signature before adding what you bought. We keep the same things as for Google, with Apple's transaction ID as the order number. Apple tells our server about refunds, and we take back what the purchase gave. Restore Purchases in Settings gives back No Ads and the Starter Pack.

Notifications

Reminders (a chest is ready, the daily gift) are scheduled on your phone. No notification server is involved. You can turn them off in Settings (ALERTS), or in the phone's settings.

No phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The app doesn't ask for those permissions, and they are explicitly blocked in its configuration. Your name and email reach us only if you log in with Facebook (and allow the email), or if you write to us. From Play Games we get only the player ID; from Apple only the Apple user ID. No Facebook friends, no Facebook token. No third-party crash service is used.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT → DELETE. Your account, game, Play Games, Facebook and Apple links and match records are deleted right away, and you get a receipt code. The game starts again as a new guest. With Facebook or Apple the account is shared: deleting it also deletes your Last Sheep Royale progress on that account.

From Facebook: remove «SetteGames» in your Facebook settings (Apps and websites) and ask to delete your data. Facebook tells us, we delete the account linked to that Facebook in all our games, and Facebook gives you a code to check it on our deletion status page (SetteGames privacy policy).

Sign in with Apple: deleting the account revokes the game's access at Apple. If Apple doesn't answer, we keep only the token, without your account, and retry the revocation.

By email: write to supporto@settegames.com with the ID shown at the bottom of Settings and, if you bought something, the Google Play order number (GPA.…) or the App Store order ID. We delete the account within thirty days. More on the delete page.

What stays: the purchase code, pack, Google order number, status and dates of a purchase, with no link to you or your device. If Google refunded a purchase or we fixed your account by hand, a short note of it stays in our internal records. The anonymous statistics are not linked to your account: to erase them too, send us the installation ID.

Backups. For safety we keep a few copies of the database on a company computer, not shared with anyone. Deleted data disappears from them when the copies are replaced: we keep only the last 14 copies.

Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings; change your consent in Settings → AD PRIVACY.

Acquisti (Google Play e App Store)

Pacchetti di gemme, Starter Pack, pass premium della stagione e No Ads si comprano con Google Play. Il pagamento lo gestisce Google: non vediamo mai la tua carta né il tuo account Google. Il gioco dice a Google l'ID del tuo account, così l'acquisto è del tuo account. Dopo il pagamento il gioco manda la conferma (quale pacchetto, e un codice d'acquisto) al nostro server, che chiede a Google se è valida e solo allora aggiunge quello che hai comprato.

Il server tiene codice d'acquisto, pacchetto, numero d'ordine di Google, identificativo d'installazione, account, stato e data, perché un acquisto non si usi due volte e per aiutarti se segnali un problema (legittimo interesse: prevenzione delle frodi). Tiene il tuo indirizzo IP solo in memoria, per dieci minuti, contro gli abusi. Se Google rimborsa un acquisto, togliamo quello che aveva dato.

Su iPhone si compra dall'App Store: il pagamento lo gestisce Apple, e non vediamo mai la tua carta né il tuo ID Apple. Il gioco dice ad Apple l'ID del tuo account (come codice casuale), riceve la ricevuta firmata da Apple (quale pacchetto, l'ID della transazione) e il nostro server controlla la firma di Apple prima di aggiungere quello che hai comprato. Teniamo le stesse cose che per Google, con l'ID della transazione di Apple come numero d'ordine. Apple avvisa il nostro server dei rimborsi, e togliamo quello che l'acquisto aveva dato. Restore Purchases in Settings ti ridà No Ads e lo Starter Pack.
