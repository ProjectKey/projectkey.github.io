Snake 100 Battle Royale — Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · version 1.1.0 (test version)

In short. In this version of the game your progress stays on your phone: there is no account on our server, no sign-in, and the game sends nothing about you or your matches to us. The other snakes in a match are played by your phone. Google shows ads (with your consent where the law requires it) and handles payments; Expo delivers small game updates. We don't sell data. To erase your game, uninstall the app or clear its data.

Who is responsible

The data controller is PK - PROJECT KEY S.R.L., Via Città della Pieve 76, 00191 Roma (RM), Italy — VAT and tax code 15538561000, REA 1597981.

For anything about your data write to supporto@settegames.com. We answer within thirty days.

What stays on your phone

The game keeps these things only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are not sent to us:

  • your snake's name, the one you choose in the game (it passes through a bad-words filter on the phone);
  • your game: coins, gems, items, level, chests, pass, missions, league, daily gift and streak, tutorial done;
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration, alerts, controls, Training difficulty;
  • an installation ID: a random number created on first launch, shown in Settings. It is not your device ID or your advertising ID.

If something in the app breaks, the error is noted on the phone; it is not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, erases all of the above. We can't restore it, because we don't have a copy.

If you write to us

In the game, Settings → CONTACT US and REPORT A PROBLEM open an email to our support, already filled in with what helps us: the app version, phone model and Android version, and the installation ID. You can read and change it before sending. We receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to answer you. We keep the conversation for as long as needed to help you, and at most 24 months.

App updates (Expo)

At every launch the game asks Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA), the service that delivers small updates to the game without going through the store, whether a new version is available. Expo receives a random ID created by the update system on your phone, the app version and update channel, and — like any server — your IP address. It processes them on our behalf, only to deliver updates. Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and fixed.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner on the home screen, and video ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward. There are no ads during a match. No Ads, a one-time purchase, removes the banner; reward videos stay, always optional.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information, the ad software's own diagnostic and crash data, and how you interact with ads, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google does this as an independent controller, under its own policies.

Legal basis. Personalised ads, and reading or storing the advertising ID and other information on your phone for ads, are based on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and art. 122 of the Italian privacy code). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for it with Google's consent form (UMP) before any ad is requested, and never during your first match. If you say no, you may get non-personalised ads, based only on context (the app, the approximate area from the IP address): these are shown on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game free, and only to the extent your choices in the form allow; otherwise you get no ads. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time in Settings → AD PRIVACY (shown when the form applies to you); this doesn't affect what was done before. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

Gem packs, the Starter Pack, the premium season pass and No Ads are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment: we never see your card or your Google account. The game receives from Google only the confirmation of the purchase (which pack, and a purchase code). In this version it is not sent to our server.

Notifications

Reminders (a chest is ready, the daily gift) are scheduled on your phone. No notification server is involved. You can turn them off in Settings (ALERTS), or in Android settings.

Google, as an independent controller

Google (Google Ireland Ltd and Google LLC: AdMob ads, Google Play purchases and the Play Store) receives data directly from the app under its own privacy policy: it is not our processor. It may process data in the USA and other countries; those transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC is certified, or by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. If the app crashes inside Android itself, Google Play may show us anonymous crash statistics (Android vitals).

What we don't collect

No account, name, email (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. The app doesn't ask for those permissions, and they are explicitly blocked in its configuration. No gameplay statistics leave your phone in this version, and no third-party crash service is used.

Deleting your data

Your game is only on your phone: uninstall the app, or clear its data (Android settings → Apps → Snake 100 → Storage → Clear data). There is no account on our server to delete in this version.

Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings; change your consent in Settings → AD PRIVACY.

Emails you sent us: ask us at supporto@settegames.com and we delete them within thirty days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or erase your data, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent (personalised ads), you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → AD PRIVACY, without affecting the processing done before. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the one where you live.

Children

The game is meant for players aged at least 14 (the age of digital consent in Italy, art. 2-quinquies of the Italian privacy code and art. 8 GDPR), or the age of digital consent in your country if higher, and is not directed at children. It contains ads and real-money purchases. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes

Later versions will save your progress on our server and add online matches. Before that happens we will update this policy and tell you in the app. The date at the top is always the latest version.

Cosa resta sul telefono

Il gioco tiene queste cose solo sul tuo telefono, nella memoria privata dell'app. Non ci vengono mandate:

  • il nome del tuo serpente, quello che scegli nel gioco (passa da un filtro delle parolacce sul telefono);

Puoi leggerla e cambiarla prima di mandarla. Riceviamo il tuo indirizzo email e quello che scrivi, e li usiamo solo per risponderti. Teniamo la conversazione per il tempo che serve ad aiutarti, al massimo 24 mesi.

A ogni avvio il gioco chiede a Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA), il servizio che consegna i piccoli aggiornamenti del gioco senza passare dallo store, se c'è una versione nuova. Base giuridica: il nostro legittimo interesse a tenere il gioco funzionante e corretto.

Pubblicità (Google AdMob)

Il gioco mostra un banner nella schermata principale, e video pubblicitari solo quando scegli tu di guardarne uno per un premio. Durante le partite non c'è pubblicità. No Ads, un acquisto una tantum, toglie il banner; i video con premio restano, sempre facoltativi.

Gli annunci li serve Google AdMob, che raccoglie l'ID pubblicità del dispositivo, l'indirizzo IP (posizione approssimativa), informazioni sul dispositivo, i dati di diagnostica e di arresto anomalo del proprio software, e come interagisci con gli annunci, per pubblicità, analisi e prevenzione delle frodi. Google lo fa come titolare autonomo, secondo le sue regole.

Base giuridica. Gli annunci personalizzati, e la lettura o la memorizzazione sul telefono dell'ID pubblicità e di altre informazioni per gli annunci, si basano sul tuo consenso (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR e art. 122 del Codice privacy). Nello Spazio economico europeo, nel Regno Unito e in Svizzera lo chiediamo con il modulo di consenso di Google (UMP) prima di chiedere qualunque annuncio, e mai durante la prima partita. Se dici di no, puoi ricevere annunci non personalizzati, basati solo sul contesto (l'app, la zona approssimativa dall'indirizzo IP): li mostriamo per il nostro legittimo interesse a tenere il gioco gratuito, e solo nei limiti delle scelte fatte nel modulo; altrimenti niente annunci. Puoi revocare o cambiare il consenso quando vuoi in Settings → AD PRIVACY (compare quando il modulo vale per te); la revoca non tocca quello che è stato fatto prima. Puoi anche reimpostare o cancellare l'ID pubblicità dalle impostazioni del telefono. Come Google usa i dati: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Acquisti (Google Play)

Pacchetti di gemme, Starter Pack, pass premium della stagione e No Ads si comprano con Google Play. Il pagamento lo gestisce Google: non vediamo mai la tua carta né il tuo account Google. Notifiche

I promemoria (un baule pronto, il regalo del giorno) sono programmati sul telefono. Nessun server di notifiche. Puoi spegnerli nelle impostazioni del gioco (ALERTS) o in quelle di Android.

Google, titolare autonomo

Google (Google Ireland Ltd e Google LLC: annunci AdMob, acquisti e Play Store) riceve dati direttamente dall'app secondo la sua informativa: non è un nostro responsabile. Può trattarli negli USA e in altri paesi; quei trasferimenti sono coperti dall'EU-US Data Privacy Framework, a cui Google LLC è certificata, o dalle Clausole contrattuali tipo dell'UE. Se l'app si blocca dentro Android, Google Play può mostrarci statistiche anonime degli arresti anomali (Android vitals).
