Snake 100 Battle Royale

100 snakes. One arena. Be the last one.

How to play

  • Eat. Grow longer.
  • Grab a weapon. Pistol: 6 shots. Laser: 3.
  • Line them up. It fires when a rival crosses your nose. Hit snakes lose tail.
  • Cut in. Make them crash into you.
  • Stay in the zone. It shrinks.

Matches last 2 to 4 minutes. One finger is enough.

The other snakes are computer-controlled. Free, with optional purchases: nothing for sale helps you win.

New: skins and heads

  • Missions. Finish 5, 15, 30, 60 to win Explorer, Detective, Robot, Astronaut.
  • Ten new heads. Kitty, Bee, Diver, Zombie, Vampire, Unicorn and more.
  • Profile. Swap your look in one tap.
  • Chests. Above Wood, almost every chest holds a common skin.
